# Dark Theme Ecommerce Store
## Made a fully functional front-end Ecommerce website but in Dark Mode.

## Description:
### Its an Ecommerce store website made with Html Css and JavaScript, which is also in Dark theme. In this Ecommerce website you can even track your orders and it also has a payment method option but still needs a payment gateway or you can say a proper backend to get this website into the real business.

## Why i made this?
### I am always eager to challange myself into these type of challenging builds that requires a lot of time and effors, Its not my longest project but it stills comes in my most time taking projects! I have also made this by watching a long [tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dfWADTiq-M4&t).

## How to order yourself?
### When you are landed on the main page click on the shop now button that will lead you to the product categories, or you can even search from the search bar for your favourite product, if the website will have the product it will be seen on your search results. when you select a category it will lead you to the products related to the category, and than you can add to cart or even 'buy now' any product you want, if you click on add to cart it will be added to your cart and will be saved to local storage and when ever you go to your cart or even you close the website and open it later your item will be saved in your cart, unless you remove the item from your cart or place order, if you click on place order it will show you checkout page and than when you proceed it will ask you payment options and when you confirm the payment method the order will be confirmed and it will give you a order id, than you can see your previous orders on the orders page.
